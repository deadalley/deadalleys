#### Features

- Finds the games you have in Steam, Xbox (Microsoft Store / Game Pass), GOG, Epic Games, EA, Ubisoft Connect and Battle.net automatically, with no logins
- Filter by launcher and by installed or not installed, search by name, and sort by name, last played or most played
- Group games by launcher or by collection. Steam collections and GOG Galaxy tags are imported, and you can make your own
- Two layouts: a grid of covers, or games lined up spine by spine like cases on a real shelf, with four shelf styles and PlayStation- or Xbox-style cases
- Launch any game from the shelf, or open its launcher's install page if it isn't installed
- Edit any game's name, cover, launch command and collections, and add any other game yourself with playtime tracking
- Fully playable with an Xbox controller or any standard gamepad
- Light and dark mode

#### Available for

- Desktop (Windows)
